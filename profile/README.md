### What GROM is

GROM is a wallet-first trading interface. You connect your own wallet, pick a market and sign every transaction yourself. There is no account, no sign-up and no deposit: funds never sit on a GROM balance.

| Product | What you can do | Open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Swap & bridge** | Swap 10,000+ tokens on the same network or across networks in one flow. GROM compares routes from several aggregators and shows fees and the expected output before you sign. | [grom.exchange/swap](https://grom.exchange/swap) |
| **Trade** | Perpetual futures on crypto majors from a connected wallet. | [grom.exchange/futures](https://grom.exchange/futures) |
| **Predictions** | Event markets on crypto, economics and world events. | [grom.exchange/predict](https://grom.exchange/predict) |
| **Stocks** | Tokenized stocks and ETFs, bought on-chain with crypto. | [grom.exchange/stocks](https://grom.exchange/stocks) |
| **Markets** | Live prices, trending DEX tokens and new listings. | [grom.exchange/markets](https://grom.exchange/markets) |

### Routing and networks

Swaps are routed through **LI.FI, Velora (ParaSwap), KyberSwap, Squid** and **Jupiter** on Solana. Supported networks include Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche, Linea, Solana, Robinhood Chain, HyperEVM, Monad, Plasma and X Layer. A configured provider does not guarantee a route for every pair and amount — the in-app quote is the reference.

### Principles

- **Self-custody.** Every trade is signed in your wallet; GROM never holds your funds or keys.
- **Transparent costs.** Fees, network gas and the expected output are shown in the quote before you sign.
- **No account.** Connect a wallet and go. Third-party services and some regions may apply their own checks.
